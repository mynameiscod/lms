B · Skill Assessment & Onboarding — the flagship funnel
🧭

AI Skill Assessment

An auto-composed, adaptive exam that scores readiness and generates a plan.
Key features
  • Segment-aware registration (fresher → job-switcher)
  • WhatsApp OTP → auto-creates a Student account
  • AI exam designer + generator (MCQ, predict-output, debug, live-code, SQL)
  • Code-verified questions run on Piston; adaptive difficulty
  • Readiness score, percentile, per-skill gap map
Target audience
Working professionalsFinal-year studentsJob-switchers
Outcome: a warm, pre-qualified lead who has seen exactly where they stand — the moment they're most ready to buy.

AI Roadmap & Result Page

The #1 conversion surface — turns a score into a personalized, sellable plan.
Key features
  • Claude-generated roadmap matched to a mentor master-track
  • Personalized: mastered areas compress, weak areas expand
  • Readiness gauge, salary-potential, gap breakdown
  • Auto-enrol as a free preview + dual CTA (buy / talk to mentor)
  • Login details emailed & WhatsApp'd automatically
Target audience
Assessed candidatesMentors / counsellors
Outcome: candidate → paid learner via Razorpay self-serve or a mentor-assisted close.
