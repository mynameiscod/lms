CodeBegun · Product Vision

The Placement Bridge: we don't send resumes, we send proof.

You've trained hundreds of job-ready developers. Companies are desperate for pre-screened headcount they can interview today. CodeBegun is the bridge — a two-sided placement engine where every candidate arrives with verified evidence of their skill.

🎓 Supply: trained, screened students
🏢 Demand: companies needing headcount
🔗 Bridge: match · prove · interview · place
🛡️ Moat: verified proof, not resumes
The Opportunity

You already own both sides of a marketplace

The two hardest things in any placement business are (1) a reliable supply of good candidates and (2) demand from companies. You have both. The gap is that they aren't connected yet — the pieces exist as islands.

🎓 The Supply side — your students

Hundreds of developers who've completed structured training and been assessed: DSA, Spring Boot, React, SQL, live coding, mock interviews, communication. This is inventory most placement agencies would kill for — and it's pre-qualified.

🏢 The Demand side — hiring companies

Companies constantly need fresher/junior headcount and are buried in unqualified resumes. They want a trusted, curated channel that sends them 2–3 people worth interviewing — not 200 to sift. That trusted channel is what you become.

The Moat

Your unfair advantage: proof, not paper

Anyone can email a company a resume. What no job board and no agency can do is hand an HR manager verifiable evidence that a candidate is actually good — and you generate all of it automatically, every day.

Every CodeBegun candidate already produces:

✅ Assessment readiness score + percentile ✅ AI mock-interview grade ✅ Communication score ✅ Real project work + GitHub score ✅ Resume + verified certificate
Here's a resume, please review → Here's proof this person can do the job — interview them today
Why this wins: HRs don't fear a bad resume — they fear wasting an interview slot. The moment your shortlist means "vetted, low-risk, worth my time," companies stop treating you as a vendor and start treating you as their hiring partner. That trust is the whole business.

The Ideas

7 moves to make the bridge great, ranked

#1 · START HERE

Verified Candidate Profile

A shareable link per student that aggregates readiness %, mock-interview grade, top project (live demo/GitHub), communication score, resume — the trust artifact an HR opens in 5 seconds. It's what makes companies say yes.

Effort: MediumImpact: Very High
#2 · CORE OF THE BRIDGE

Request-Candidates → AI shortlist

On an opening, "Request candidates" → AI matches placement-ready students → you approve → send 2–3 verified profiles. The matching you deferred, now powered by assessment data + your AI infra.

Effort: MediumImpact: Very High
#3 · THE BACKBONE

Close the loop: Placement → Interview → Feedback → Placed

Wire outreach into the Interviews module: schedule, capture HR decision + reason, mark placed, trigger 90-day guarantee. Turns islands into one operational spine and gives you data nobody else has.

Effort: MediumImpact: High
#4 · PROTECT THE BRAND

"Placement-Ready" gating + badge

Only students who clear a bar (mock interview passed + readiness ≥ X + communication ≥ Y) are surfaced to companies, with a badge they earn. One bad candidate burns an HR relationship — this protects it and motivates students.

Effort: LowImpact: High
#5 · THE NORTH STAR

Company / HR self-serve portal

A light company-facing view: see anonymized shortlists, request interviews, give feedback themselves. Turns manual outreach into a self-serve channel. Bigger build — do it after #1–#3 prove the model.

Effort: HighImpact: High (long-term)
#6 · COMPOUNDING MOAT

Feedback → training flywheel

Interview rejection reasons feed back into curriculum ("3 failed on system design → add a module"). Over a year, your students measurably out-perform every other institute's. This is the moat that compounds.

Effort: MediumImpact: Compounding
#7 · FILL THE PIPELINE

Demand generation on autopilot

Extend openings discovery: monitor job boards for companies hiring your students' stack in your city → auto-queue outreach. Plus alumni referrals — your warmest channel.

Effort: MediumImpact: High
Roadmap

A phased build that reuses what you have

Phase 1 The proof + the match — the minimum that makes it a real bridge

  • Verified Candidate Profile — plumb assessment + mock-interview + communication + project data into one shareable link.
  • Request-Candidates → AI shortlist — match placement-ready students to an opening, approve, send verified profiles.

Phase 2 Close the loop

  • Wire shortlist → schedule interview → capture feedback → mark placed → guarantee.
  • Add the Placement-Ready gate + badge so only strong candidates surface.

Phase 3 Scale demand + the flywheel

  • Auto-discover hiring companies; activate alumni referrals.
  • Feed interview feedback into curriculum + student prep.

Phase 4 Self-serve company portal

  • Let HRs browse anonymized shortlists, request interviews and give feedback themselves — the long-term marketplace.

⚙️ The daily loop for your placement team (once built)

1

3

So you can run Moves 1–2 right now — Phase 1 just makes Move 3 (the proof shortlist) one click instead of manual.
