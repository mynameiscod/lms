4 steps from "company is hiring" to "our candidate is hired"

1

2
Reach out with our pitch Live now

Send the branded CodeBegun intro — who we are, our job-ready students, no fee to evaluate. Follow-ups go automatically.

3
Send our candidate — with proof To build

Share 2–3 candidate profiles that show real evidence: assessment score, mock-interview grade, project work, communication — so HR sees the person is worth interviewing.

4
They interview → they accept To build

Schedule the interview, track it, and mark the candidate placed. That's the win.

Steps 1–2 already work today. The only thing that turns this into a repeatable "our candidates get accepted" engine is Step 3 — sending proof, not a plain resume.
Why companies say yes

We send proof, not paper

An HR won't waste an interview slot on an unknown resume. They will interview someone who arrives with evidence they can do the job — and our students already produce all of it.

Every CodeBegun candidate already has:

✅ Assessment score ✅ Mock-interview grade ✅ Communication score ✅ Real project + GitHub ✅ Resume + certificate
Here's a resume, please review → Here's proof — interview them, no risk
This is the whole point. When your shortlist means "vetted, low-risk, worth my time," companies interview your students — and come back for more.
What to build (just this)

One thing: the Candidate Proof Profile

Keep it lean. The single piece that makes candidates get accepted is a shareable profile that gathers the proof you already have into one link an HR opens in 5 seconds.

📄 Candidate Proof Profile

One shareable link per student: assessment score, mock-interview grade, top project (live/GitHub), communication score, resume. This is what you send in Step 3.

🎯 Pick candidates for a role

For a company's opening, choose 2–3 fitting students and send their proof profiles. (Later, AI can suggest the best fits automatically — not needed to start.)

Nothing else is required right now — no portal, no dashboards, no marketplace. Just: find hiring company (done) → send proof of our candidate → they interview → hired.
How you use it

Your placement team's daily loop

  • Morning: open Placement Partnership → Add-by-company → find companies hiring your students' stack (see their openings).
  • Reach out: send the branded intro to the HR contact; the system follows up for you.
  • On reply: pick 2–3 fitting students → send their proof profiles (the one part to build).
  • Close: schedule the interview, track it, mark placed.
