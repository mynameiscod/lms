CareerPilot, end to end

A consumer career product running alongside the LMS on shared infrastructure but with its own identity, its own signup, and its own paid tier. This is every feature it has, what an admin controls, and what a student does — traced against the deployed routes rather than the plan.

What it is. A stranger arrives at /passport/join with no LMS account. They verify a phone number, take a free assessment, and are shown a career readiness score. Paying ₹499 unlocks a 90-day plan and five working tools. Nothing here requires them to be a CodeBegun student — which is the point: it is a separate funnel that happens to share a codebase.

The student journey

Numbered because each stage genuinely gates the next — a member cannot reach step 5 without completing step 3.

1
Landing & signupfree · public
GET /passport/join · POST /public/passport/signup

Name, phone, email. No LMS account needed. The page states the product's claims and its price up front rather than after signup.

2
WhatsApp OTP verificationfree
POST /public/passport/verify · /resend

Phone ownership is proven before anything is created. Returning members can instead sign in by password or fall back to OTP — /login-password, /login-otp.

3
Career assessmentfree
GET /passport/assessment · POST /assessment/submit

A deterministic, non-AI assessment across weighted categories. Scoring is reproducible: the same answers always produce the same result, so two students comparing notes never see a contradiction.

4
Result & the paywallfree to view
GET /passport/assessment/result

Career score, per-category breakdown, weakest areas, and a recommended pathway. A 7-day preview of the roadmap is shown with later phases visible but empty — deliberately, so the preview cannot be mistaken for the whole product.

5
Membership — ₹499paid
POST /passport/membership/order · /membership/verify

Razorpay, on the LMS's existing payment rail with purpose: passport_membership. Both popup and redirect modes are handled, including the case where payment completes in a tab that has lost the app.

6
Mission Controlmember home
GET /passport/dashboard · /missions/today · POST /missions/complete

Three missions each day, chosen deterministically from the student's weakest categories plus one rotating focus. Completing them drives XP and a streak shared across every CareerPilot surface.

7
Public proof cardpublic link
GET /passport/card/:slug

A shareable page carrying verified scores and progress — the artefact a member sends to a recruiter instead of a claim in a CV.

The six member features

What ₹499 actually buys. Verification status is stated per feature, because "deployed" and "known to work" are different things.

Daily Missions

verified
/missions/today · /missions/complete

Three tasks a day from admin-editable pools, biased to the member's weakest categories. Deterministic per day and slot, so reloading never reshuffles the day's work. Feeds XP and streak.

90-Day Roadmap

verified
/roadmap

Three phases, weekly themes and goals, every day resolved to the exact missions that day will serve. Locked to a 7-day preview until membership is active.

Practice Lab

SQL verified only
/practice · /practice/:id/run · /submit

Coding, SQL and MCQ problems executed on self-hosted Piston with hidden tests. The SQL path was verified end-to-end against production; the coding path has not been exercised.

Mock Interview

never executed
/interview/start · /:id/turn · /:id/finish

A turn-based AI interview with scored feedback. Fully deployed and, across roughly 25 releases, never run by a member or by us.

Resume Centre

never executed
/resume · /resume/score · /resume/improve

Build, score against ATS-style criteria, and get AI improvements. Same status as the interview: shipped, unproven.

Progress & Streak

verified
/dashboard

One XP total and one streak across missions, practice and interviews — a single number rather than a per-feature score, so effort anywhere counts once.

Who controls what

The dividing line: admins control the content and the rules; students control their own effort and their own data. Neither side can reach into the other's column.

AdminControls the product
Pricing & configuration Membership price, currency, feature toggles, journey length. /passport/config — manage_passport only.
Mission pools Every daily task, per category: title, detail, type, XP value, link. Editing the pool changes what members receive tomorrow. /passport/content
Pathways Career tracks, weekly themes and the descriptions that shape a member's roadmap.
Assessment bank Questions, categories, weights and scoring bands. /assessment/admin
Member list Who joined, who paid, where they are. Read-only under view_passport_members.
Grant membership without payment Convert a member manually. convert_passport_member — deliberately separate from viewing, because it moves money.
Preview before publishing /content/preview renders what a member would receive, so pool edits are checked before they reach anyone.
StudentControls their journey
Join without an LMS account Self-serve signup and phone verification. No admin approval step.
Take the assessment Free, and repeatable only if an admin resets it — results cannot be quietly re-rolled.
Buy or decline The free tier stays usable. Declining leaves the assessment result and the 7-day preview intact.
Complete missions Mark today's three done. Cannot alter which missions appear — that is set by the pools and their own weakest categories.
Practise and be graded Run and submit against hidden tests. Scores are computed server-side; the client is never trusted.
Own their resume Create, edit, score and improve. It belongs to the member, not the tenant.
Publish or withhold the card The public proof link is theirs to share. Nothing is exposed until they choose.

Permission matrix

Four permissions, deliberately split so that seeing a member's data never implies being able to give away a paid product.

PermissionGrantsAdminStaffInstructorStudent
manage_passportConfig, price, pools, pathways, assessment bank✓———
view_passport_membersRead the member list and progress✓✓——
convert_passport_memberGrant membership without payment✓———
use_passportUse CareerPilot as a member———✓

Why the split matters. Before it existed, staff who could view members could also change the ₹499 price and grant free memberships. Reading a list and giving away a paid product are not the same act, and one role should not carry both by accident.

Status, stated plainly

The funnel is complete and live. Two paid features inside it are not proven.

Mock Interview and Resume Centre have never been executed — not by a member, not by us, across roughly 25 deployments. They typecheck, they are routed, they are behind the paywall. Nobody has confirmed they run. Practice Lab was in the same position until its SQL path was executed end-to-end against production Piston this week; the coding path still is.

  • Run each once. Ten minutes settles whether a ₹499 entitlement works. This is the single highest-value action on the module.
  • Confirm the landing-page figures. The 12,000+ students / 500+ colleges / 98% satisfaction claims sit beside testimonials that turned out to be mockup copy and were removed. The numbers were confirmed as accurate; they deserve the same scrutiny the testimonials got.
  • Decide the assessment reset policy. Only an admin can reset an attempt today. That is defensible, but it should be a stated rule rather than an implementation detail.
