Skill Assessment Module — End-to-End

A self-serve, AI-powered assessment that turns cold ad traffic into warm, pre-qualified, paying learners. The exam is the lead magnet; the money is the personalized roadmap + LMS access. Every step is designed to move a candidate from "just curious" to "ready to enrol."

platform.codebegun.com/assessment/<tenant>?segment=<segment>

What this module is

A candidate clicks a segment-tagged ad, lands on a branded assessment page, and — with just an email and a WhatsApp OTP — takes an adaptive, AI-composed exam tailored to their profile. In minutes they get a Readiness Score, a gap map, a salary-potential estimate, and a personalized day-by-day roadmap — all backed by a real LMS account that's already been created and pre-enrolled in a free preview of their plan. From there they convert online (Razorpay) or via a mentor call. The institute gets a fully-scored, ready-to-close lead with zero manual effort.

The end-to-end flow

Nine stages, fully automated — from ad click to paying learner.

1

Ad → Landing Prospect

A segment-tagged ad (Meta / Google) links to the public assessment page for the tenant. The page is branded and mobile-first; no login needed to start.

Segment auto-detected from URLPer-tenant brandingPlacements / Blog / About nav
2

Registration Prospect

Lightweight form captures who they are and what they want — this drives the entire personalized exam and roadmap.

Name · EmailProfile: B.Tech year / Graduate / ProfessionalPrimary languageTarget role & salaryCurrent stack & experienceResume upload (optional)
3

WhatsApp OTP verification Prospect

A 6-digit code is sent over WhatsApp (Cloud API, approved template). On verify, a real Student LMS account is auto-created and login details are emailed & WhatsApp'd.

WhatsApp Cloud APIApproved OTP templateAuto account creationDev-mode code fallback
4

AI Exam Designer System · Claude

Claude designs a per-candidate blueprint from the profile + parsed resume. Freshers get aptitude/fundamentals-heavy exams; seniors get system design and harder DSA (no aptitude).

Profile + résumé awareSegment-specific dimensionsDevice-aware (mobile-friendly)
5

AI Question Generation System · Claude + Piston

Claude writes every question across all item types. Code questions (predict-output, live-code, SQL) are validated by running the reference solution on Piston — broken items are discarded, so candidates only see verified questions.

6 item typesPiston-verified outputsBackground bank pre-warm
6

Adaptive delivery Prospect

The exam is delivered in stages; difficulty shifts on the running score. Keyboard-only tasks become optional on phones and are back-filled with tap-friendly items — zero drop-off on any device.

Adaptive difficultyAnti-cheat guardsMonaco code editorMobile back-fill
7

Scoring System

Responses are scored per dimension into a single Readiness Score plus a percentile vs peers in the same segment, and a per-skill gap map.

Readiness %Percentile vs peersPer-dimension breakdown
8

AI Roadmap + auto-enrol System · Claude

The candidate is matched to a mentor master-track, which is cloned and personalized (mastered areas compress, weak areas expand, ≥90% drops). They're auto-enrolled as a free preview (days 1–2 open); the rest is teased.

Master-track match by role+levelPersonalized clonePreview enrollmentCRM lead sync
9

★ Result Page → Convert Prospect → Learner

The #1 conversion surface: readiness gauge, salary potential, gap map, personalized plan preview and a dual CTA. Buy now → instant full unlock (Razorpay); Talk to a mentor → routed to the team for a human close.

Readiness gauge + bandSalary deltaRoadmap previewRazorpay unlockMentor / CRM route
